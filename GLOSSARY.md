# Project-to-print terms

## Source filament

A filament referenced by a project file. A plate uses a particular set of source filaments, each with its own identity within the project.

## Filament requirement

The material characteristics requested for a source filament on a selected plate. A requirement describes what the job needs; it does not identify a loaded physical spool.

## Hive catalog filament

A filament product recorded in Hive's catalog. Its name, brand and SKU describe the product, while its material type and color describe characteristics that can satisfy a requirement.

## Target slot

A physical filament route on the printer chosen to run the job. Smart Queue can determine this route when it chooses a printer and matches the job's requirements.

## Saved slice usage

A record of source filaments involved in a previous slicing of a plate. It can differ from the current project's filament usage.
